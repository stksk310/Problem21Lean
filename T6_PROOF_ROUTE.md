returned coordinates vanish by removal inside the same factorization, while
their distinguished-generator levels are positive because those directions
are missing. Comparing the actual equations with the canonical singleton row
gives the signed JR and KR identities.

direction-0 coefficients are capped uniformly by the exhaustive sign partition

factorizations of the same `fA`; positive theta uses the actual `A_k` gap;
zero/zero is handled separately; and the remaining three regions contradict a
missing singleton direction or the singleton gap itself.

whose coefficients are nonnegative by the endpoint and uniform caps. Both are
actual factorizations of the Frobenius gap.
