Region U is closed by uniform I/J capacity bounds and repeated replacement
inside one actual upper element. Region D first yields sharp crossing caps and
the scale-aware multiplicity master. Strict multiplicity excludes `h ≥ 2`;
prefix minimality excludes the nonlinear first point when `h = 1`.

The surviving linear case supplies D-LINEAR and INTRINSIC-LINEAR. Its positive
discriminant gives exact quotient/remainder parameters. The committed 715-term
table is regenerated byte-for-byte, its polynomial identity is proved by Lean,
and coefficientwise nonnegativity proves the I-fit threshold. A repeated actual
packet closes the j-shortage branch, yielding the complementary packet.

The two packets are embedded in positive matrix entries `p,q,v,t` with
`p*t-q*v=1`, `L>M`, positive abstract packet determinant, and exact rho
identities. `toEuclideanSeed` removes all first-point state. `c9_handoff`
eliminates the U side of the frozen C8 disjunction and maps D to the seed.
