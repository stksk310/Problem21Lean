## 1. Numerical tail from symmetry

Setting gives positive and irredundant tail generators and nonnegative values
of every actual natural coefficient vector. If symmetry is at f, every tail gap
is at most f. Thus all integers at least f+1 lie in the tail. Consecutive actual
tail factorizations show every common divisor divides 1, and their difference
gives a signed Bezout identity used only for divisibility.

Least positive critical multiples also exist, with actual off-direction natural
coefficient witnesses. Their coefficients are at least two by irredundancy.
These foundations are verified but the shorter classification spine below does
not require a general relation or Betti-element library.

## 2. Apéry top and residue classes

Fix x=n(0), y=n(1), z=n(2). Write Ap(H,x) for actual h in H with h-x outside H.
Symmetry gives top t=f+x and the involution h ↦ t-h on Ap(H,x).
Every actual representation of an Apéry member has zero x-coordinate.

Each residue 0≤r<x has a least nonnegative actual representative in H. This
representative lies in Ap(H,x). Two Apéry members with equal residue must be
equal: their difference is a nonnegative multiple of x, contradicting the gap
condition if nonzero. Hence |Ap(H,x)|=x, with no parameter-bounded search.

## 3. Multiple representations of the top

Suppose t has two distinct nonnegative y,z representations. Divide y,z by their
positive gcd d, obtaining primitive u,v. Two-generator representation difference
forces one representation to contain at least v copies of y or u copies of z.
Consequently their least common multiple d*u*v is below t in the H-order and
belongs to Ap(H,x).

If x were outside <u,v>, two-generator Frobenius symmetry would give
uv-u-v-x=a*u+b*v with a,b≥0. The identity

    d*u*v-x = a*(d*u)+b*(d*v)+(d*u)+(d*v)+(d-1)*x

would be an actual nonnegative representation, contradicting the Apéry gap.
Therefore x belongs to <u,v>, with nonnegative witnesses.

## 4. Unique representation of the top

Otherwise write t=A*y+B*z uniquely, with A,B≥0. Complementation implies that
Apéry members are precisely the values a*y+b*z with 0≤a≤A and 0≤b≤B.
These rectangular representations are injective, since any alternate one could
be completed to an alternate representation of t. Put L=A+1 and M=B+1.
Counting the rectangle against residue classes gives x=L*M.

Reduce L*y and M*z to their Apéry representatives. Minimality of the rectangle
bounds forces boundary relations

    L*y=q*x+b*z,   M*z=r*x+a*y,
    q,r>0, 0≤a<L, 0≤b<M.

Eliminating y,z and using the three-generator Bezout identity proves
x divides L*M-a*b. Since x=L*M and 0≤a*b<L*M, this forces a*b=0.

If a=0, exact cancellation yields z=L*r and y=q*M+b*r. Thus x,z have the common
factor L, their primitive factors M,r are coprime, and y has the required actual
nonnegative representation. The b=0 case exchanges y,z with a genuine Fin 3
permutation.
